Uganda at 64: State House Entebbe to Host Virtual Independence Celebrations Under New Sovereignty Theme

The national milestone marks 64 years since the historic lowering of the British Union Jack in 1962. In light of current state priorities, the government has transitioned the official event to a virtual format at State House Entebbe. This shift limits the physical guest list to a select group of VVIPs and invited dignitaries while redirecting public attention to a broader national discussion. By centering the celebrations on the theme of sovereignty and visionary leadership, the state aims to encourage collective self-reliance, economic transformation, and long-term development across the country..

Johannesburg, South Africa — The Uganda Cranes fought back late to secure a 1-1 draw against the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) in an international friendly match at the FNB Stadium on Monday, 5th October 2026.

A thunderous 89th-minute equalizer from substitute James Bogere cancelled out Nathanael Mbuku’s second-half opener, ensuring Uganda walked away with a hard-earned result. DR Congo Dominates Early Exchanges The Leopards of DRC controlled the tempo for most of the match, enjoying superior ball possession and carving out several half-choices in the first period. Despite their dominance, the Ugandan backline held firm to keep the score goalless heading into the break. Uganda return out stronger in the second half and missed a golden opportunity to break the deadlock in the 49th minute. Forward Arnold Vula found himself in space inside the goal area but dragged his close-range effort wide of the target. Mbuku Punishes Cranes' Defensive Lapse DR Congo finally translated their dominance into a goal in the 69th minute. St. Truiden forward Nathanael Mbuku Wa Mbuku intercepted a loose pass from Cranes defender Arnold Odong. Mbuku capitalised on the error, unleashing a low 20-yard drive that beat Ugandan goalkeeper Kingdom Osayi to give the Leopards a 1-0 lead.
