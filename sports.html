Uganda Cranes build momentum ahead of AFCON

HOIMA, Uganda — The Uganda Cranes have built considerable momentum for the upcoming AFCON 2027, capping off a stellar unbeaten run during the September–October international window under head coach Paul Put. As a co-host for the historic Pamoja AFCON 2027 tournament alongside Kenya and Tanzania, Uganda has a guaranteed spot but is treating the qualifiers as a vital testing ground to build a title-contending squad.

Basketball

Basketball is rapidly becoming one of the most popular sports in Uganda. What started as a minor school game has grown into a massive national movement, packing local stadiums and drawing global attention. At the center of this boom is the National Basketball League (NBL), where intense rivalries like those between the City Oilers and the Namuwongo Blazers draw thousands of passionate fans. Beyond domestic courts, Uganda’s national teams—the Silverbacks (men) and the Gazelles (women)—are making major waves in international FIBA and 3x3 tournaments.

Rugby

The Rise of the Oval Ball in Uganda In Uganda, rugby is a rapidly growing sport that captures the nation's passion for high-energy, physical competition. Managed by the Uganda Rugby Union, the game has evolved from its colonial-era roots into a mainstream sporting phenomenon, heavily centered around vibrant hubs like the Kyadondo Rugby Grounds and Legends Rugby Club in Kampala.

Uganda Cranes

National Football Team

The Uganda Cranes are Uganda's national football team and are managed by the Federation of Uganda Football Associations (FUFA). The team represents Uganda in the Africa Cup of Nations, World Cup qualifiers and other international competitions.

The Cranes have qualified several times for the Africa Cup of Nations and remain one of East Africa's most recognised football teams.

Other Popular Sports

Besides football, Uganda excels in athletics, boxing, rugby, cricket and netball.

Ugandan athletes have earned international recognition in long-distance running competitions and world championships.

Future of Sports in Uganda

New sports facilities, youth academies and international tournaments are helping develop young talent across the country.
