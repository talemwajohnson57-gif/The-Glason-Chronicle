Discover Uganda

The Pearl of Africa

Uganda is known as the Pearl of Africa because of its beautiful landscapes, diverse wildlife and rich cultural heritage.

The country is home to mountain gorillas, the source of the Nile, national parks and vibrant cities such as Kampala and Jinja.

Ugandan Culture

Uganda has more than 50 ethnic communities, each with unique traditions, languages, music and dances.

Traditional ceremonies, cultural festivals and local crafts remain an important part of daily life.

Popular Foods

  • Matooke
  • Luwombo
  • Rolex
  • Posho and Beans
  • Groundnut Sauce

These dishes are enjoyed throughout Uganda and form an important part of the country's culinary identity.

Travel Tips

  • Visit Bwindi National Park.
  • Explore the Source of the Nile.
  • Experience cultural performances at Ndere Centre.
  • Enjoy local markets in Kampala.
